## §5 NEW — The reframe (it's the system, not the people)

**H2: It's not your people. It's the system they work in.**

Think about it: the same employee who "lacks initiative" in your office
somehow organises a wedding for 300 guests on the weekend. People don't lack
ability — they follow whatever system surrounds them. And when the workplace
has no system, they follow the oldest rule there is: *wait for the boss to
chase.*

That's why the usual fixes keep failing:

- **The motivational talk.** Everyone claps. Two weeks later the energy is
  gone, because Monday morning still works exactly like before.
- **KPIs and dashboards.** People learn to explain the numbers instead of
  owning the outcome.
- **Hiring more staff.** Adding people to a team with no system just gives
  you more people to chase.
- **Replacing people.** New hires inherit the same chaos — and produce the
  same results within 90 days.

High-performing teams in banks, airports and tech companies don't run on
talent or motivation. They run on three boring-sounding things:

- **SOPs** that live on paper, not in one employee's head
- **Ownership** so clear that nobody waits to be chased
- **Workflows** everyone can see — so "I thought he was doing it" dies

*(vocabulary matches the ad: "SOPs · Ownership · Workflows")*

A system is not a personality trait. It can be **installed**. That's what
Creating Super Teams does — in 2 days, with your real team, on your real work.

## §14 FAQ — expanded from 4 to 7

Existing four kept (HRD Corp claim / small teams / not lecturing / cost).
Three new:

**"We're too busy for 2 days of training."**
That's usually the strongest sign you need it. The busyness is the symptom —
chasing, rework and fire-fighting consume far more than 2 days every month.
And because the workshop runs on your team's real projects, the work doesn't
stop for the training: your team keeps moving it forward *inside* the room.

**"We've done team training before. Nothing changed."**
Most training fails the Monday-morning test: inspiring on the day, zero
change to how work actually flows. Creating Super Teams doesn't teach
concepts and hope. It installs a concrete system — ownership, visible work, a
delivery rhythm — onto your team's actual projects during the workshop, and
your team leaves with a 30-day plan they built themselves. What changes on
Monday is decided before Day 2 ends.

**"My industry is different — will this work for us?"**
The system isn't industry-specific: it works anywhere work arrives, gets
divided among people, and has to ship. Ethan has installed it in banks,
airports, manufacturers, government agencies and tech companies. Because the
2 days run on your team's real work, it fits your industry by construction —
and if we honestly think your case isn't a fit, we'll tell you that on the
free 15-minute call.
